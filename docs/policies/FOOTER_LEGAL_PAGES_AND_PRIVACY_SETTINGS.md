# Footer, Legal Pages and Privacy Settings

**Status:** Requirements documented; implementation and legal review remain separate tasks.

The shared responsive footer must display `© {current year} KhelGrid. All rights reserved.`, links for About, Contact, Privacy, Terms, IPR & Copyright, Cancellation, POSH Policy, Editorial, Verification, Corrections and Sitemap, plus a visible Privacy & Cookie Settings control.

## Social links
Show a “Follow KhelGrid” or “Community” section using only confirmed official profiles. The known Reddit community URL is https://www.reddit.com/r/Khelgridonline/. Other handles must be confirmed; never invent URLs or link unrelated brands.

## Privacy settings
Provide accessible controls for necessary versus optional categories, accept/reject granular choices, persist choice/version/time as appropriate, reopen/change/withdraw consent, and prevent optional scripts from loading before valid consent where required. Controls must reflect actual integrations and have a safe fallback.

## Legal pages and sitemap
Create substantive, accessible pages with accurate claims and contact details only after they are confirmed. Do not fabricate a legal entity, committee, address, certification or operational policy. Generate the sitemap from canonical public routes only.

## Social sharing and sourcing
Public pages may offer native share/copy-link controls; never post automatically to social accounts. Social posts are leads, not automatic verification. Respect platform terms, privacy, copyright and source restrictions. Define moderation before enabling user-generated content, comments or messaging.

## Acceptance
All links resolve; footer is keyboard/mobile accessible; privacy choices persist and can be changed; optional scripts respect choices; social URLs are verified; legal content reflects actual behaviour and receives appropriate review. This document does not mean the pages or settings are implemented.
