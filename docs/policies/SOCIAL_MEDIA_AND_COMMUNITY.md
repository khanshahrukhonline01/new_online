# Social Media and Community Policy

**Status:** Draft; confirm official profiles before publication

## Official links
Use only profiles confirmed by the project owner. The known Reddit community link is https://www.reddit.com/r/Khelgridonline/. Label it as the KhelGrid Reddit community. Never invent handles or link to unrelated brands.

## Sharing
Public pages may provide native share or copy-link actions. Do not post automatically to a user's social account. Ensure shared titles/previews are accurate and avoid leaking private or unpublished information.

## Content sourcing
Social posts may be leads for editorial review, not automatic proof. Prefer official source pages; respect platform terms, copyright, privacy and access restrictions. Record provenance and independently check dates, eligibility and status.

## Moderation
Before enabling user submissions, comments or messaging, define prohibited content, reporting, review, appeal, escalation, retention and emergency response. Do not imply moderation exists before it is operational.

## Privacy
Inventory social embeds, pixels and widgets. Apply consent rules where required; do not load optional tracking before valid consent where applicable.
