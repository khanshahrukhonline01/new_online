# Editorial, Verification, Corrections and Intellectual Property

**Status:** Draft policy baseline · Requires owner review before public-policy claims.

## Source and provenance
For every public opportunity/event record, retain the original source URL, publisher, capture/review date, relevant evidence, editor/reviewer and last-checked timestamp where available. Prefer the primary organizer or official federation/institution source. Secondary reports may help discover a listing but must not be represented as official confirmation.

## Publication and status
Distinguish draft, pending review, published, unverified, expired, cancelled and removed states. Do not label a listing verified unless the documented evidence and authorized review support that label. Never invent dates, fees, venue, eligibility, contact details, prize money or availability.

## Freshness and corrections
Define review intervals by record type. Re-check deadlines and status as events approach; automatically flag stale records for review and expire or unpublish them according to an approved policy. Provide a correction channel and retain an appropriate audit trail without exposing private reporter details.

## Copyright and reuse
Link to original sources and summarize in original wording. Do not copy full articles, photographs, logos, videos, PDFs or database contents without permission or a valid licence/legal basis. Record licence/permission and attribution requirements for each asset. Public accessibility does not imply permission to republish.

## Verification integrity
Define evidence, reviewer role, timestamp, scope and expiry for each verification badge. Make clear what was checked and what was not. Provide a correction/appeal process. Paid placement or sponsorship must not be represented as independent verification.

## Takedown and complaints
Provide a documented intake, acknowledgement, evidence review, temporary restriction where appropriate, decision, appeal and record-retention process. Escalate credible safety, privacy, impersonation or rights complaints. Restrict access to complainant information.

## Acceptance checklist
- [ ] Public claims match evidence and visible source links.
- [ ] Unverified and expired records are clearly distinguished.
- [ ] No copyrighted assets are reused without recorded rights/permission.
- [ ] Corrections and takedowns have an owner and response workflow.
- [ ] Verification labels state a defensible scope and review date.
- [ ] Legal review is completed before presenting this draft as an adopted legal policy.
