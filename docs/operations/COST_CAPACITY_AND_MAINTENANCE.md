# Cost, Capacity and Maintenance Register

**Status:** Draft

List hosting, database, domain, email, storage, monitoring, data providers, ads and payments. Track free-tier quotas, bandwidth, build minutes, storage, egress and rate limits. Do not introduce paid dependencies without owner approval.

Record traffic assumptions and test critical paths. Establish thresholds for quota warnings and upgrade decisions. Do not promise scalability without measurement.

Assign an owner and review cadence for dependencies, content freshness, broken links, security advisories, backups, legal policies and provider terms.

For each subsystem record owner, backup contact/role, provider dashboard, documentation, renewal date, recovery path and escalation. Never put secrets in this register. Review costs before paid services, alert before quotas/renewals, and test backup recovery.
