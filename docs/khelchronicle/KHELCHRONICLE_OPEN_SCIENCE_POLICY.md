# KhelChronicle Open Science, Research Integrity & Preprint Repository Policy

- **Repository Platform**: KhelChronicle / SportRxiv India
- **Publisher / Operator**: KhelGrid Open Sports Science Foundation
- **Document Version**: 2.0.0 (International Open Science & Crossref Alignment)
- **Effective Date**: November 2026
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- **Affiliated Standards**: Society for Transparency, Openness, and Replication in Kinesiology (STORK), Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE), International Committee of Medical Journal Editors (ICMJE), FAIR Data Principles.

---

## 1. Mission and Core Principles

KhelChronicle is India's dedicated Diamond Open Access sports science, biomechanics, exercise physiology, sports medicine, and athletic performance preprint repository. 

Our mission is to:
1. **Accelerate Scientific Priority & Discovery**: Provide authors with immediate timestamped priority of discovery and persistent Crossref DOIs without waiting months for traditional journal publishing cycles.
2. **Eliminate Financial Barriers (Diamond Open Access)**: Guarantee 100% free deposit (Zero Article Processing Charges / APCs) and 100% free reader access with no paywalls or subscriptions.
3. **Bridge Elite Science and Grassroots Field Coaching**: Translate complex kinematic, physiological, and normative benchmarks into actionable field cues for physical education teachers, grassroots academy coaches, and sports practitioners across India and worldwide.

---

## 2. Diamond Open Access & Intellectual Property (CC BY 4.0)

1. **Zero Fees**: Neither authors nor their institutions are ever charged submission, processing, page, color, or publication fees.
2. **Copyright Retention**: Contributing authors retain full copyright of their intellectual property.
3. **License Terms**: All manuscripts, normative benchmarks, and supplementary assets are published under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)**:
   * **Share**: Anyone may copy and redistribute the material in any medium or format.
   * **Adapt**: Anyone may remix, transform, and build upon the material for any purpose, including commercial coaching frameworks and analytics tools.
   * **Attribution**: Proper credit must be given to the original authors, a link to the license provided, and an indication if modifications were made.
4. **Permanent Public Archive**: By depositing on KhelChronicle, authors grant the platform a perpetual, non-exclusive, irrevocable license to archive, distribute, and preserve the manuscript and its associated bibliographic metadata worldwide.

## 3. Human Subject Ethics, Minor Safeguarding & Informed Consent

KhelChronicle enforces strict ethical standards for all empirical, observational, and experimental investigations involving human subjects:

1. **Declaration of Helsinki & ICMR Alignment**: Studies must comply with the World Medical Association Declaration of Helsinki and the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) *Ethical Guidelines for Biomedical Research on Human Participants*.
2. **Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC/IRB) Approval**:
   * Every manuscript involving human physical testing (e.g. motion capture, force plates, high-altitude hypoxia, blood lactate, maximum aerobic testing, biological age radiography) must explicitly state the approving Institutional Ethics Committee, approval protocol number, and date of clearance.
3. **Minor Athlete Safeguarding (Under 18 Years)**:
   * Empirical testing involving junior, sub-junior, or grassroots academy athletes (e.g. U-14, U-16, U-18 cohorts) requires documented written informed consent from parents or legal guardians, along with participant informed assent.
   * Testing protocols must comply with Indian child protection statutes (including POCSO guidelines) and sports federation junior safety mandates.
4. **Vulnerable Populations**: Research involving tribal youth talent schemes, para-athletes, or clinical patient cohorts must document specialized participant welfare and vulnerability protections.

## 4. FAIR Primary Data, Code & Supplementary Materials

In accordance with international Open Science principles, KhelChronicle mandates adherence to the **FAIR Principles** (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable):

1. **Data Availability Statements**: Every deposited manuscript must include a standalone *Data Availability Statement* detailing where primary data can be accessed.
2. **Public Data Repositories**: Authors are strongly encouraged to deposit de-identified raw datasets in certified open research archives:
   * **Zenodo**: General scientific data, force-plate time series, and 3D coordinate matrices.
   * **Open Science Framework (OSF)**: Study protocols, preregistration dossiers, and lab procedures.
   * **Dryad / Figshare**: Supplementary high-throughput tables.
   * **GitHub / Zenodo DOI**: Reproducible Python, R, or MATLAB analysis pipelines and predictive algorithms.
3. **Restricted Data Justification**: If data cannot be shared publicly due to athlete medical privacy, proprietary commercial IP, or national team confidentiality, the authors must state the exact legal/ethical grounds and provide contact criteria under which qualified researchers may request access.

## 5. Conflict of Interest & Financial Transparency

Transparency in funding and commercial relationships is essential for research credibility:
1. **Declaration of Competing Interests**: All authors must declare any financial, commercial, or personal interests related to sports gear manufacturers, supplement companies, commercial academies, private leagues, or betting entities.
2. **Funding Statements**: Explicit disclosures must name all funding agencies, government sports grants (e.g. Khelo India, Sports Authority of India, LNIPE, CSIR, DST), university fellowships, or private sponsors.

## 6. Preprints, Version of Record (VoR) & Journal Compatibility

1. **Ingaution Prior to Journal Publication**: Major sports science and medical publishers—including *British Journal of Sports Medicine (BMJ)*, *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise (ACSM)*, *Journal of Sports Sciences (Taylor & Francis)*, *Elsevier*, and *Springer-Nature*—permit preprint deposition prior to or during journal review.
2. **Permanent Crossref DOIs**: Each preprint receives a permanent Digital Object Identifier (e.g. `10.51224/khelchronicle.2026.001`).
3. **Version of Record (VoR) Cross-Linking**: When a preprint is accepted and published in a peer-reviewed journal:
   * Authors should notify KhelChronicle or update the metadata with the formal journal DOI.
   * KhelChronicle will display a prominent emerald **Version of Record (VoR) Published** banner directing readers to cite the formal journal edition while preserving the open access preprint permanently.

## 7. Versioning, Corrections & Withdrawal Policy

1. **Permanent Scientific Record**: Preprints form part of the permanent citable literature and cannot be removed simply upon author request.
2. **Revisions and Changelogs**: Authors may deposit updated versions (e.g. v1.1, v2.0) to incorporate community feedback or post-peer-review corrections. Each revision is timestamped and accompanied by a public changelog.
3. **Withdrawal and Retraction SOP**:
   * Preprints will only be formally withdrawn or retracted in extraordinary cases involving:
     * Documented scientific misconduct or data fabrication.
     * Severe ethical violations or lack of human subject consent.
     * Copyright infringement or legal court order.
   * Following COPE standards, the preprint text will be replaced with a formal public *Withdrawal Notice* explaining the rationale, while the persistent DOI, title, and author metadata remain preserved for bibliographic audit integrity.

---

## 8. Community Peer Review & Scientific Discourse Conduct

1. **Open Scientific Dialogue**: KhelChronicle hosts open, attributed pre-publication and post-publication peer reviews, methodological inquiries, and field replication observations.
2. **Attribution and Identity**: Reviewers must provide their full name, verified ORCID iD, and institutional/academy affiliation. Anonymous or pseudonymous comments are not permitted.
3. **Code of Conduct (COPE Alignment)**:
   * Comments must focus strictly on scientific methodology, statistical validity, data transparency, and practical field implications.
   * Ad hominem attacks, derogatory commentary, discriminatory remarks, and commercial promotional spam are strictly prohibited and will be moderated.
   * Reviewers must disclose any competing interests related to the paper or its authors.

## 9. Machine-Readable Syndication, RSS 2.0 & PRISM Feeds

To empower research libraries, universities, sports science institutes, reference managers (Zotero, Mendeley), and automated bibliometric indexing harvesters, KhelChronicle exposes a live, machine-readable syndication feed:

1. **Feed Specification**: Conforms to the RSS 2.0 standard augmented with Dublin Core 1.1 (`xmlns:dc="http://purl.org/dc/elements/1.1/"`) and PRISM 2.0 (`xmlns:prism="http://prismstandard.org/namespaces/basic/2.0/"`) vocabularies.
2. **Canonical Endpoint**: [`https://khelgrid.com/khelchronicle/feed.xml`](https://khelgrid.com/khelchronicle/feed.xml)
3. **Syndicated Metadata Attributes**:
   - `dc:creator`: Author name, ORCID identifier, and institutional affiliation.
   - `dc:date` & `prism:publicationDate`: Timestamped publication and revision dates.
   - `dc:identifier` & `prism:doi`: Permanent Crossref DOI resolution links.
   - `prism:section`: Academic kinesiology and sports discipline classification.
   - `content:encoded`: Structured background, methodology, empirical results, and conclusion abstracts.
   - `enclosure`: Direct link to open access PDF galleys.
