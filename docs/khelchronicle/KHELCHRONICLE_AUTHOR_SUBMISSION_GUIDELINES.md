- **Publisher**: KhelGrid Open Sports Science Foundation

## 1. Eligible Subject Disciplines & Scope

KhelChronicle welcomes original scientific preprints, empirical field protocols, systematic reviews, benchmark athletic datasets, and high-performance case studies across seven core disciplinary pillars:

1. **Sports Biomechanics & Kinematics**: High-speed motion analysis, ground reaction forces, force-plate telemetry, wearable IMU sensor validation, bowling/pitching kinematics, running economy, and equipment mechanics.
2. **Exercise Physiology & Conditioning**: Cardiorespiratory fitness, aerobic/anaerobic thresholds, high-altitude acclimatization, lactate kinetics, thermal stress, hydration dynamics, and recovery protocols.
3. **Sports Nutrition & Metabolism**: Macronutrient periodization, micronutrient deficiencies in Indian athletes, hydration strategies, energy availability (RED-s), and ergogenic supplementation.
4. **Sports Psychology & Mental Conditioning**: Cognitive resilience, perceptual-motor decision making, anxiety regulation, mental fatigue, visualization, and grassroots coach communication.
5. **Sports Medicine & Injury Epidemiology**: Injury surveillance registers, ACL/lumbar stress risk factors, return-to-play protocols, concussion assessment, and rehabilitation biomechanics.
6. **Sports Analytics & Machine Learning**: Computer vision tracking, predictive performance modeling, automated talent identification algorithms, match tactical telemetry, and scouting analytics.
7. **Sports Development & Grassroots Policy**: Physical literacy frameworks, synthetic turf equity, school physical education infrastructure, talent development pathways, and national sports policy evaluation.

## 2. Manuscript Structure & Preparation

All deposited manuscripts should be prepared in PDF format with standard academic typography (e.g. 11–12pt font, 1.5 line spacing, numbered pages and lines).

### Required Manuscript Elements

1. **Title & Running Head**: Clear, descriptive academic title (and optional subtitle).
2. **Author Byline & Affiliations**:
   * Full names of all contributing authors.
   * Departmental and institutional/academy affiliations.
   * Verified ORCID iD for each author (e.g. `https://orcid.org/0000-0002-8941-4412`).
   * Designated corresponding author with academic email.
3. **Structured Abstract (Maximum 350 Words)**:
   * **Background**: Athletic performance problem, clinical question, or scientific gap.
   * **Methods**: Cohort size ($N$), participant demographics, experimental design, instrumentation, and statistical tests.
   * **Results**: Primary quantitative findings with exact values, effect sizes ($d$, $\eta^2$), confidence intervals ($95\%\text{ CI}$), and significance levels ($p$).
   * **Conclusion**: High-level scientific takeaway and implications for sport.
4. **Keywords**: 4–8 relevant indexing keywords, including sport-specific terms.
5. **Grassroots Coaching Takeaways (Bilingual Recommended)**:
   * 3–5 bullet points summarizing direct, practical takeaways for physical education teachers, grassroots academy coaches, and athletes in plain English and Hindi.
6. **Full Text Sections**:
   * **Introduction**: Literature review, rationale, and specific hypotheses.
   * **Methods**: Detailed, reproducible protocol, calibration steps, and statistical analysis plans.
   * **Results**: Empirical narrative supported by formal academic tables and publication-quality figures.
   * **Discussion**: Interpretation of findings, biomechanical/physiological mechanisms, practical field applications, and study limitations.
7. **Mandatory Research Statements**:
   * **Institutional Ethics Statement**: Ethics committee clearance number and date.
   * **Participant Consent Statement**: Written informed consent and minor assent confirmation.
   * **Data Availability Statement**: Persistent URL or DOI to raw data.
   * **Code Availability Statement**: Link to open scripts/software pipeline (if applicable).
   * **Funding Disclosures**: Explicit funding source or statement of no financial support.
   * **Conflicts of Interest**: Mandatory declaration of any commercial or consultancy ties.
8. **References**: Formatted in APA 7th Edition style with complete Crossref DOIs whenever available.

## 3. Supplementary Data & Open Science Badges

KhelChronicle awards international Open Science Badges displayed prominently on the preprint masthead:

* 🟢 **Open Data**: Primary raw telemetry, force-plate CSV files, or kinematic coordinates deposited on Zenodo, OSF, or Dryad.
* 💻 **Open Materials / Code**: Reproducible scripts (Python, R, MATLAB) or CAD hardware designs shared on public GitHub or Zenodo repositories.
* 📝 **Preregistered Study**: Study design, hypothesis, and statistical sampling plans preregistered on the Open Science Framework (OSF) or ClinicalTrials.gov prior to data collection.

## 4. Submission Checklist

Before submitting on the KhelChronicle portal, authors must verify:
- [ ] Manuscript is complete, original work in sports/movement science.
- [ ] All authors have approved submission and agree to the CC BY 4.0 Diamond Open Access license.
- [ ] Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC/IRB) approval number is documented.
- [ ] Written informed consent has been collected for all human participants (and parental consent for minors).
- [ ] All author affiliations and ORCID iDs are provided.
- [ ] Structured abstract and coaching field implications are included.
- [ ] Data availability statement and supplementary files are prepared.
