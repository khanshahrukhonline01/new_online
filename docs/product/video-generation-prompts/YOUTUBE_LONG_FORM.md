## Narrative outline

| Segment | Planning range | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Hook | 0:00–0:20 | Establish relevance and curiosity |
| Introduction | 0:20–0:50 | Define the subject and scope |
| Chapter 1 | As needed | Explain the first key idea |
| Chapter 2 | As needed | Develop evidence or demonstration |
| Chapter 3 | As needed | Add practical context or implications |
| Summary | Final 30–60 sec | Reinforce the main takeaway |
| Closing | Final 5–15 sec | Relevant KhelGrid identity / CTA |

## Production guidance

- Open with the viewer's question, not a lengthy brand animation.
- Use chapter cards sparingly and add them in post-production.
- Alternate presenter, demonstration, diagrams and supporting visuals.
- Keep examples relevant and fact-checked.
- Avoid padding, repeated points and unsupported claims.
- Add reviewed captions and chapter timestamps.

## Shot list

Create one shot entry per visual using the fields in the master framework. Plan separate 16:9 framing and safe areas for titles and captions.

## Final review

Check narrative completeness, sources, sports accuracy, pacing, audio intelligibility, graphics, captions, rights and final export.
