## Pre-interview requirements

- Explain the intended platforms, audience, editing and possible reuse.
- Obtain documented informed consent and image / voice permissions.
- Agree how names, personal details and sensitive information will be handled.
- Confirm permissions for training venues, teams, coaches and third-party material.
- Do not promise outcomes, exposure, selection or commercial benefits.

## Interview structure

1. Introduction in the athlete's own words.
2. Early interest and sporting context.
3. Training and development experience.
4. Challenges and lessons, as the athlete chooses to share.
5. Goals and advice.
6. Closing reflection.

## Production safeguards

- Do not fabricate dialogue, achievements, rankings or personal history.
- Do not use synthetic voice or face replacement to alter the contributor without explicit informed permission.
- Avoid intrusive questions and unnecessary personal data.
- Give contributors a clear contact route for consent or privacy concerns.
- Use b-roll that genuinely represents the story; label illustrative scenes appropriately.

## Final review

Confirm consent scope, name and pronunciation, quote accuracy, contextual fairness, footage permissions, music rights, captions and final edit approval.
