## Research and evidence plan

For every factual claim, record the source, publication date, relevant passage or data, and verification status. Separate:
- Established facts
- Attributed opinions
- Unresolved questions
- Illustrative reconstructions
- AI-generated scenes

Do not use AI-generated visuals to imply that an unfilmed event, interview or historical moment was actually recorded.

## Story structure

1. Opening scene and central question.
2. Historical or sporting context.
3. Main developments and evidence.
4. Voices / perspectives, with consent and accurate attribution.
5. Challenges, limitations or competing interpretations.
6. What the evidence supports and what remains uncertain.
7. Conclusion and source / further-reading direction.

## Editorial safeguards

- No invented interviews, quotes, archival footage or participant experiences.
- Obtain releases for interviews and identifiable contributors.
- Respect privacy, context and fair representation.
- Label illustrative reconstructions clearly where viewers could mistake them for genuine footage.
- Attribute archival and third-party footage according to licence and editorial standards.
- Review sensitive claims and allegations carefully before publication.

## Final documentary review

A researcher/editor must verify the evidence trail, timeline, attributions, permissions, captions, audio rights, visual rights and any required synthetic-media disclosures.
