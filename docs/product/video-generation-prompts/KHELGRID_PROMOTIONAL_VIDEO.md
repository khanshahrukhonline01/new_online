## Claims and product verification

Before scripting, list every product statement:
- Exact claim:
- Evidence / live product location:
- Current status: live / limited / planned / unavailable
- Approved wording:
- Reviewer:

Only describe features that are actually available and verified. Label planned or upcoming features clearly. Do not claim verified listings, partnerships, bookings, payments, live scores, user numbers or outcomes without evidence.

## Suggested structure

1. Audience problem or need.
2. KhelGrid's verified role in addressing it.
3. Demonstration of the actual live experience.
4. Clear limitations or eligibility details where relevant.
5. Simple, accurate call to action.

## Screen-recording / demonstration checklist

- Use the current approved site or product build.
- Remove personal, private or test information.
- Do not fabricate users, listings, notifications, reviews or transactions.
- Check links and displayed data before recording.
- Capture current mobile and desktop layouts as needed.
- Keep the recording date and product version in the production notes.

## Brand treatment

Use approved KhelGrid logo and brand colours in post-production. Keep the message helpful and specific; avoid exaggerated claims, pressure tactics or unsupported comparisons.

## Approval

Product owner verifies feature accuracy; editorial reviewer verifies claims; rights reviewer checks footage, music and third-party assets; brand reviewer approves final graphics and CTA.
