# KhelGrid Video Generation and Scripting Prompt Library

**Status:** Draft for review  
**Purpose:** Reusable briefs and production templates for distinct KhelGrid video categories.

## How to use

1. Read the [Master AI Video Generation and Production Framework](../MASTER_AI_VIDEO_GENERATION_AND_SCRIPTING.md) first.
2. Select the category template that matches the intended video.
3. Complete its project brief, audience outcome, script and shot list.
4. Verify factual claims and source material before generation.
5. Generate or film shots, then edit and review them.
6. Complete rights, editorial and brand approvals.
7. Record the final asset in the [Video Asset Metadata Template](VIDEO_METADATA_TEMPLATE.md).

## Templates

- [YouTube Long-form](YOUTUBE_LONG_FORM.md) — structured videos with chapters and a developed narrative.
- [YouTube Shorts](YOUTUBE_SHORTS.md) — one focused idea in a vertical, concise format.
- [Sports Documentary](SPORTS_DOCUMENTARY.md) — research-led storytelling about a sport, topic or sporting ecosystem.
- [Sports Explainer](SPORTS_EXPLAINER.md) — clear educational breakdowns, rules, techniques or processes.
- [Athlete Story](ATHLETE_STORY.md) — permission-led profiles and first-person stories.
- [KhelGrid Promotional Video](KHELGRID_PROMOTIONAL_VIDEO.md) — accurate platform and brand communication.
- [KhelChronicle Editorial Video](KHELCHRONICLE_VIDEO.md) — research, analysis and editorial explainers.
- [Video Asset Metadata](VIDEO_METADATA_TEMPLATE.md) — generation, source, rights and approval records.

## Production rules

- These templates do not authorize publication or establish rights.
- Do not invent real events, athlete biographies, quotes, statistics, partnerships, listings or platform capabilities.
- Clearly distinguish fictional illustrative visuals from genuine footage.
- Keep the original script, prompts, source references, raw clips and project files.
- Mark rights and production approval as pending until reviewed.
- Adapt duration and pacing to the story; example timings are editorial guidance, not fixed rules.
