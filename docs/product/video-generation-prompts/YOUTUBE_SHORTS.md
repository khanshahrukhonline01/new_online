## Vertical editing rules

- Keep the key subject central and crop-safe.
- Keep captions away from interface overlays and screen edges.
- Use large, readable captions with strong contrast.
- Make the idea understandable without sound where practical.
- Use fast cuts only when they improve comprehension.
- Avoid tiny charts, dense paragraphs and excessive transitions.
- Produce a native vertical edit; do not simply crop a horizontal master.

## Quality gate

The Short must communicate one idea accurately, use verified claims, have readable captions, clear audio, rights-cleared assets and a strong opening that does not mislead.
