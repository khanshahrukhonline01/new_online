# KhelGrid — Master AI Image Generation Prompt

**Status:** In Review  
**Purpose:** Reusable prompt standard for generating original KhelGrid sports imagery.  
**Applies to:** Homepage hero images, sport landing pages, editorial illustrations and other approved platform visuals.  
**Related document:** [Asset Manifest and Rights Workflow](ASSET_MANIFEST_AND_RIGHTS_WORKFLOW.md)

Use this template as the starting prompt for each sport. Replace the bracketed fields in Section 2 and make only sport-specific adjustments needed for accurate rules, equipment, uniforms and environments. Keep the brand, originality, legal and technical safeguards intact.

---

## 1. Brand identity

Create an original, premium-quality sports photograph-style image for KhelGrid, an Indian sports discovery and athlete-development platform.

KhelGrid's visual identity:

- Primary colour: deep navy.
- Secondary colour: emerald green.
- Supporting colours: natural sporting colours, white and restrained neutral tones.
- Visual personality: energetic, ambitious, authentic, inclusive, professional and inspiring.
- Overall aesthetic: cinematic sports photography with realistic environments and believable sporting action.

## 2. Image subject

Generate an image featuring:

**Sport:** [INSERT SPORT]

**Environment:** [INSERT STADIUM / SPORTS GROUND / ARENA / COURT]

**Action:** [INSERT SPECIFIC SPORTING ACTION]

**Athlete:** Fictional, non-identifiable athlete or athletes. Use realistic sporting clothing and equipment appropriate to the sport.

**Location style:** Indian sporting environment where appropriate, without falsely depicting a specific real-world venue.

## 3. Photography and composition

- Photorealistic, high-quality sports photography.
- Capture a compelling sporting moment with natural body mechanics.
- Use wide-angle environmental composition where suitable.
- Show the playing surface, sporting equipment and surrounding environment clearly.
- Use cinematic lighting appropriate to the sport.
- Create depth through realistic foreground, subject and background separation.
- Maintain natural skin textures, realistic proportions and anatomically correct athletes.
- Keep the main subject visually clear.
- Avoid excessive visual effects, artificial-looking textures and oversaturated colours.

## 4. KhelGrid visual treatment

- Integrate deep navy and emerald green naturally into the overall composition.
- Use subtle brand-colour accents in appropriate environmental details.
- Keep the image premium, clean and recognisable as part of the KhelGrid visual family.
- Avoid overwhelming the photograph with graphic overlays.
- Preserve a suitable area of negative space for website headlines and interface elements.
- Do not place important subjects where responsive cropping may remove them.

## 5. Branding instructions

- Do not reproduce any existing sports club, league, federation, sponsor or company logo.
- Do not use real team kits, protected trademarks or unauthorized brand marks.
- Do not create fake official sponsorships or endorsements.
- Do not insert text, slogans, watermarks or fabricated event names into the image.
- Leave branding and text overlays to KhelGrid's website interface.
- Use the official KhelGrid logo separately when an approved graphic composition requires it.

## 6. Legal and originality safeguards

- Create a new composition rather than recreating an existing photograph.
- Do not imitate a named photographer, living artist or specific copyrighted image.
- Do not depict recognizable real athletes without the required permissions.
- Avoid copyrighted characters and identifiable protected designs.
- Do not falsely represent the image as a real match, event, venue or sporting achievement.
- Generate fictional sporting environments when no verified venue photograph is available.

## 7. Technical requirements

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 for homepage hero images.
- Preferred master resolution: 1920 × 1080 or higher where supported.
- Composition must support desktop and mobile cropping.
- No embedded text.
- No unnecessary borders or frames.
- Save the original generated output.
- Produce optimized WebP derivatives for website delivery.
- Preserve sufficient image quality for large desktop displays.

## 8. Negative prompt

Avoid:

- Distorted faces, hands, limbs or sporting equipment.
- Incorrect sporting rules, court layouts or equipment.
- Unrealistic athlete anatomy or impossible body positions.
- Duplicate athletes or malformed crowds.
- Gibberish text and accidental logos.
- Fake sponsor boards and fabricated official branding.
- Excessive artificial glow, oversaturated colours and cartoon effects.
- Watermarks, signatures and stock-photo marks.
- Composition that makes the sporting action difficult to understand.

## 9. Final output

Generate one original image that:

1. Clearly communicates the selected sport.
2. Looks like premium professional sports photography.
3. Fits the KhelGrid navy-and-emerald visual identity.
4. Has a strong, memorable sporting moment.
5. Can be used as a website visual after rights and quality review.

Return the image without embedded promotional text or logos.

## Usage and approval notes

- Keep a copy of the exact prompt submitted, the original generated output and any edited derivative.
- Record the generation service/model, date, output identifier and terms applicable to the account and date of generation.
- AI generation alone does not establish exclusive copyright ownership, non-infringement or production approval. Follow the [Asset Manifest and Rights Workflow](ASSET_MANIFEST_AND_RIGHTS_WORKFLOW.md).
- A human reviewer must check sporting accuracy, visual quality, responsive crops, trademarks, likeness/privacy concerns and rights evidence before production use.
- Until the required review is complete, mark the asset **Rights review: Pending** and **Production approval: Pending**.
