## Prompt

Create a photorealistic, premium sports photograph for KhelGrid featuring **athletics** in a believable Indian training or competition environment.

- **Discipline:** [100 m sprint / middle-distance running / long jump / high jump / javelin / shot put / relay]
- **Action:** [describe the precise phase of movement]
- **Athlete:** Fictional, non-identifiable athlete in generic, unbranded sportswear and discipline-appropriate footwear or equipment.
- **Environment:** [standard athletics track / field-event area / training ground] with correct lane markings and event equipment.
- **Composition:** Wide 16:9 scene with the athlete's complete action visible and enough environmental context. Keep the athlete clear of crop-sensitive edges.
- **Lighting:** [morning daylight / golden hour / stadium lighting]
- **KhelGrid treatment:** Restrained navy and emerald accents in the environment, not on fabricated sponsor panels.

Make the movement biomechanically credible for the selected discipline. Show correct equipment, landing area and track/event layout.

## Avoid

Incorrect lane layout, wrong event equipment, impossible joint positions, floating athletes, unsafe or implausible technique, duplicate limbs, fabricated timing boards, real federation insignia, sponsor logos or text.
