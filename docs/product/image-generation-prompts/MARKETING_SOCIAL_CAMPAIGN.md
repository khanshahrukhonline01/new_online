# Asset prompt — Social media and campaign graphics

**Inherits:** [Master AI Image Generation Prompt](../MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_PROMPT.md)

## Prompt

Create an original KhelGrid campaign visual for **[CAMPAIGN / ANNOUNCEMENT / EDUCATIONAL POST]** focused on **[SPORT / ATHLETE TOPIC]**.

- **Channel and format:** [Instagram square 1:1 / portrait 4:5 / story 9:16 / LinkedIn landscape 1.91:1].
- **Visual subject:** [ONE clear sporting scene or visual metaphor].
- **Audience:** [ATHLETES / COACHES / ACADEMIES / VENUE OPERATORS / SPORTS COMMUNITY].
- **Composition:** Clear focal point with deliberate negative space for separately typeset copy and official KhelGrid logo.
- **Brand palette:** Deep navy, emerald green, white and natural sporting colours.
- **Style:** Consistent with KhelGrid's premium, authentic sports photography identity.
- **Output:** Background artwork only. Do not render text, hashtags, dates, QR codes, logos, sponsor marks or fabricated claims.

All campaign wording, facts, dates, URLs and logos must be added later from verified source material in the design system.
