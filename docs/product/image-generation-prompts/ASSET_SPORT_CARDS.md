# Asset prompt — Sport catalogue cards

**Inherits:** [Master AI Image Generation Prompt](../MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_PROMPT.md)

## Prompt

Create a clear, photorealistic image representing **[SPORT]** for a KhelGrid sport catalogue card.

- Show one recognizable action or the defining equipment of the sport.
- Use a simple, uncluttered [portrait / square / landscape] composition suitable for a small responsive card.
- Keep the athlete and essential equipment readable at thumbnail size.
- Use a believable sporting environment and fictional, non-identifiable athletes.
- Use subtle navy and emerald environmental accents while keeping the sport's natural colours.
- Avoid text, badges, numbers, logos, sponsor marks, collages and decorative borders.
- Keep the focal subject near the centre safe area to support responsive cropping.

The image must identify the sport at a glance and remain visually consistent with the KhelGrid image family.
