## Prompt

Create a photorealistic, neutral and inviting visual of a **[SPORT] facility** for KhelGrid's venue discovery experience.

- **Facility type:** [TURF / CRICKET GROUND / BADMINTON HALL / BASKETBALL COURT / ATHLETICS TRACK / OTHER]
- **Setting:** [INDOOR / OUTDOOR], with a plausible Indian community, academy or recreational sports context.
- **View:** [wide establishing view / court-level view / elevated architectural view].
- **Visible details:** Accurate playing surface, markings, lighting, equipment and safe circulation areas.
- **People:** [NO PEOPLE / small number of distant fictional players], with no identifiable faces.
- **Composition:** Clean, informative and realistic; show the facility rather than a dramatic athlete close-up.
- **Brand treatment:** Subtle navy and emerald accents only; no invented venue name, signage, price board or sponsor.

This is an illustrative generic facility image. Do not imply it depicts a real listed venue. Do not invent amenities, dimensions, accessibility features or certifications.
