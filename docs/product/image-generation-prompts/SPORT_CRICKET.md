# Sport prompt — Cricket

**Inherits:** [Master AI Image Generation Prompt](../MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_PROMPT.md)

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic, cinematic sports photograph for KhelGrid showing a compelling moment in **cricket**.

- **Scene:** [international-style stadium / local Indian cricket ground / academy nets / practice field]
- **Action:** [batter playing a technically believable cover drive / bowler delivering the ball / fielder taking a catch / wicketkeeper collecting the ball]
- **Athlete:** Fictional, non-identifiable cricketer in generic, unbranded cricket clothing and appropriate protective equipment.
- **Environment:** Accurate cricket pitch, crease markings, stumps, boundary and field layout for the selected format.
- **Composition:** Wide environmental 16:9 frame. Keep the athlete and ball readable, with safe negative space for website interface overlays. Avoid placing the main action at crop-sensitive edges.
- **Lighting:** [golden-hour daylight / bright natural daylight / realistic floodlit evening]
- **KhelGrid treatment:** Deep navy and emerald accents used subtly in the environment; no logos, sponsor boards or text.

Depict a physically plausible cricket action with correct bat grip, stance, protective gear and ball trajectory. Do not depict a real match, named player, real team, league or venue.

## Avoid

Incorrect wicket or crease layout, impossible bat-ball contact, malformed bat, duplicate ball, incorrect protective gear, baseball equipment, recognizable team kits, sponsor logos, scoreboards with gibberish text, fake tournament branding.
