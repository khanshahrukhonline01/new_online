# Sport prompt — Football

**Inherits:** [Master AI Image Generation Prompt](../MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_PROMPT.md)

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic, cinematic sports photograph for KhelGrid showing a compelling moment in **association football (soccer)**.

- **Scene:** [professional-style stadium / Indian community ground / academy training pitch / indoor futsal court]
- **Action:** [attacker striking the ball / goalkeeper diving for a save / midfielder passing under pressure / players contesting an aerial ball]
- **Athletes:** Fictional, non-identifiable players wearing generic, unbranded kits.
- **Environment:** Accurate football or futsal pitch dimensions and markings for the selected setting; correct goals, nets and ball.
- **Composition:** Wide 16:9 environmental frame, clear ball and player action, useful negative space for website overlays, crop-safe subject placement.
- **Lighting:** [golden hour / overcast daylight / realistic stadium floodlights]
- **KhelGrid treatment:** Subtle navy and emerald environmental accents without club colours that imitate a known team.

Show believable body mechanics, foot placement, ball contact and player spacing. Do not depict a real club, player, competition, match or venue.

## Avoid

American football equipment, incorrect goal dimensions, malformed feet, impossible ball movement, duplicate balls, offside-line graphics, real team crests, recognizable kits, sponsor logos, fake match signage or text.
