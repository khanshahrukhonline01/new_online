# Asset prompt — KhelChronicle editorial visuals

**Inherits:** [Master AI Image Generation Prompt](../MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_PROMPT.md)

## Prompt

Create an original editorial sports photograph or conceptual visual for a KhelChronicle article about **[VERIFIED ARTICLE TOPIC]**.

- **Editorial angle:** [TRAINING SCIENCE / SPORTS TECHNOLOGY / GRASSROOTS DEVELOPMENT / PERFORMANCE / SPORTS POLICY / HISTORY].
- **Subject:** [SPORT / EQUIPMENT / TRAINING ENVIRONMENT / NON-IDENTIFIABLE ATHLETE].
- **Visual approach:** [DOCUMENTARY PHOTOGRAPHY / CONCEPTUAL STILL LIFE / GENERIC DATA-INSPIRED BACKGROUND].
- **Accuracy:** Represent only concepts supported by the article. Do not fabricate a real study, dataset, competition, athlete, institution or research result.
- **Composition:** [16:9 article header / 4:3 in-article image / square social preview], with useful crop-safe space.
- **Brand treatment:** Restrained navy and emerald accents, no embedded labels or data.
- **Output:** Image only. Article title, captions, source notes, charts and citations must be added separately from verified editorial content.

Do not use generated images as documentary evidence. Clearly distinguish illustrative artwork from actual event or research photography in captions and metadata.
