# Sport prompt — Basketball

**Inherits:** [Master AI Image Generation Prompt](../MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_PROMPT.md)

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic, cinematic sports photograph for KhelGrid showing a compelling moment in **basketball**.

- **Scene:** [indoor basketball arena / outdoor Indian community court / academy practice court]
- **Action:** [player taking a jump shot / layup / defensive block attempt / rebound / dribbling past a defender]
- **Athletes:** Fictional, non-identifiable players in generic, unbranded basketball clothing and appropriate footwear.
- **Environment:** Correct basketball court markings, hoop, backboard, rim and basketball. Match the setting to the selected indoor or outdoor environment.
- **Composition:** Wide 16:9 environmental frame; show the ball and main action clearly, with negative space for website overlays.
- **Lighting:** [natural outdoor daylight / balanced indoor arena lighting]
- **KhelGrid treatment:** Deep navy and emerald accents used subtly in court surroundings.

Use believable jumping, landing, hand placement, ball control and player spacing. Avoid any recognizable real athlete, team or venue.

## Avoid

Incorrect hoop height or court markings, distorted hands, impossible ball grip, duplicate basketballs, unsafe landing posture, real team jerseys, league logos, sponsor marks, fabricated signage or text.
