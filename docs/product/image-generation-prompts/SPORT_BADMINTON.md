# Sport prompt — Badminton

**Inherits:** [Master AI Image Generation Prompt](../MASTER_AI_IMAGE_GENERATION_PROMPT.md)

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic, premium sports photograph for KhelGrid showing a compelling moment in **badminton**.

- **Scene:** [indoor badminton hall / academy court / community sports facility]
- **Action:** [player executing an overhead smash / defensive return / net shot / doubles rally]
- **Athlete:** Fictional, non-identifiable player or players in generic, unbranded badminton clothing and court shoes.
- **Environment:** Accurate badminton court dimensions, net height and posts, correct racket and feather or synthetic shuttlecock.
- **Composition:** Wide 16:9 environmental image. Make the racket, shuttlecock and body position understandable; leave safe negative space for interface overlays.
- **Lighting:** Realistic indoor sports-hall lighting with natural shadows.
- **KhelGrid treatment:** Restrained navy and emerald details in the facility, without club or sponsor branding.

Show credible racket grip, footwork, balance and shuttle trajectory. For doubles, use believable court positions and player spacing.

## Avoid

Tennis rackets or balls, incorrect net or court layout, malformed racket strings, duplicate shuttlecocks, impossible wrist angles, distorted hands, real player likenesses, logos, sponsor boards or text.
