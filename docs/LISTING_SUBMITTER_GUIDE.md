# KhelGrid Submitter & Community Suggestion Guide

Welcome to the KhelGrid Community Listing Guide! KhelGrid relies on passionate athletes, coaches, and sports organisers across India to keep our grassroots sports catalogue up-to-date.

---

## 1. How to Submit a Listing

You can suggest a sports listing at any time by visiting `/submit-listing` (or clicking **Suggest Listing** in the site header).

### Supported Categories
1. **Sports Opportunities & Trials:** Grassroots trials, scholarship programs, club tryouts, and talent combines.
2. **Sports Venues & Turf:** Multi-sport turf grounds, badminton courts, swimming pools, tennis courts, and athletic complexes.
3. **Sports Academies:** Training centers, youth football academies, cricket coaching institutions, and martial arts dojos.
4. **Coaches & Trainers:** Certified fitness trainers, sports-specific coaches, and athletic instructors.
5. **Events & Tournaments:** Local championships, open tournaments, marathons, and collegiate cups.
6. **Pickup Games:** Casual matches, open weekend games, and local community kickabouts.
7. **Sports Disciplines:** New sports or emerging regional disciplines not yet in the KhelGrid index.

---

## 2. Free submission and independent verification

Submitting a venue, coach or sports-trial listing to KhelGrid is free. There is no listing subscription, paid placement, checkout or payment requirement for submitting or reviewing these directory records. The intake service applies server-side validation and a limit of 10 submissions per client per minute. A free submission does not guarantee approval or publication.

KhelGrid checks the listing category’s relevant evidence before publication. Venue checks may include a genuine public address and facility details; coach checks may include the qualification and issuing authority; trial checks may include the organiser, official notice, dates, eligibility and application route. Evidence review is separate from editorial approval. A document upload or a submitter’s claim alone does not automatically make a listing verified.

Any athlete entry fee charged by an independent trial organiser is separate from KhelGrid’s free listing submission. If the organiser has not disclosed an athlete fee, KhelGrid should say it is not disclosed rather than assume the event is free.

## 3. Tracking Your Submission Privately

KhelGrid does **not** require you to create an account or sign in to submit a listing suggestion. Instead, when you submit a listing, the system generates two credentials:

- **Reference Number (e.g., `KHL-20261110-8A3F`):** A human-readable identifier you can use to reference your submission.
- **Private Tracking Key (64-character token):** A cryptographic key generated specifically for your device/submission.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Keep your Reference Number and Tracking Key safe!** They are displayed on the confirmation screen immediately after submission.

### How to Check Submission Status
1. Go to `/track-submission` (or `/track`).
2. Enter your **Reference Number** and **Private Tracking Key**.
3. View the real-time status of your submission:
   - `Pending Review`: Waiting for assignment to a community moderator.
   - `Under Review`: Currently being verified by our moderation team.
   - `Changes Requested`: Additional clarification or corrections needed.
   - `Approved`: Moderation complete! Scheduled for catalogue publication.
   - `Published`: Live on KhelGrid! Publicly searchable by all users.
   - `Rejected`: Did not meet verification standards or community guidelines.
   - `Unpublished`: Previously live but now retired or archived.

---

## 4. Responding to Correction Requests

If a moderator needs more details before approving your suggestion, your tracking page will show a **Changes Requested** notice along with specific instructions (e.g., "Please provide the official registration link or venue address").

To resubmit:
1. Open `/track-submission` and enter your credentials.
2. Review the **Moderator Feedback** box.
3. Use the inline **Edit & Resubmit Form** on the tracking page to update your title, details, and source link.
4. Click **Resubmit for Review**.
5. Your submission will transition to `Resubmitted` and move to the front of the moderator review desk. Your previous version is preserved in history for audit compliance.

---

## 5. Community Guidelines & Standards

To ensure your suggestion is approved quickly:
- **Include Official Verification Links:** Provide an HTTPS link to an official club website, verified social announcement, or federation press release whenever possible.
- **Provide Clear Location Details:** Always specify the Indian city and locality where the activity takes place.
- **No Unsolicited Personal PII:** Do not submit private home addresses, personal phone numbers, or private emails of individuals without explicit consent. Use official academy or venue contact channels.
- **No Spam or Duplicate Submissions:** Avoid submitting the same event or venue multiple times.
