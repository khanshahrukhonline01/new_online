# Opportunities and Selection Trials

**Status:** Planned specification

## Listing requirements
A listing should include title, sport, organiser, source URL, location, event/deadline dates, eligibility, fee only if sourced, contact path if public, last-reviewed date and publication/verification status.

## Verification workflow
Capture provenance; check source authority and relevance; confirm date, location, eligibility and application route; record review evidence; approve publication; re-review or expire as dates pass. Official sources should be preferred.

## Editorial safeguards
Do not invent trials, dates, fees, venues, contacts or eligibility. Do not label records “verified” solely because they were found online. Correct or withdraw misleading listings quickly and preserve an audit trail.

## Acceptance
Users can reach the original source; expired listings are not represented as open; uncertain claims are clearly qualified; duplicates are controlled; corrections and takedowns have a documented path.
