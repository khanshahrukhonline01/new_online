# Analytics, Advertising and Monetization

**Status:** Deferred until providers, consent behaviour and policies are approved

Record each provider's purpose, data collected, cookies/storage, transfers, consent basis, retention, terms, ad policies, cost and removal steps.

Inventory analytics, advertising, social pixels, embeds and optional scripts. Where consent is required, block optional scripts until valid consent and respect withdrawal. Settings must reflect actual integrations.

Do not place ads deceptively or contrary to network policies. Maintain useful original content, truthful claims and separation between editorial listings and paid placements. Never fabricate traffic, clicks or conversions.

Define minimal metrics and retention; never transmit personal CV data in URLs or analytics. Test consent acceptance/rejection/withdrawal and inspect network requests before activation.
